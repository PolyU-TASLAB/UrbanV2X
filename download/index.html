Dataset structure

Small Loop · hkstp_s_01 ↗ Large Loop · hkstp_l_01 ↗

Both sequences use the following layout. Small Loop also includes camera/metadata.jsonl for the image viewer.

hkstp_s_01/ · hkstp_l_01/
├── camera/
│   ├── cam0/ … cam6/       *.png
│   └── fisheye/            *.png
├── lidar/
│   ├── hdl32/              *.bin
│   └── xt32/               *.bin
├── radar/
│   ├── Target_Radar_1/
│   └── Target_Radar_1_extreme/
├── gnss/
│   ├── messages.jsonl
│   ├── observations.obs
│   └── rinex_summary.json
├── roadside/
│   ├── rsi2/               bags/, lidar/
│   └── gnss/               *.obs
├── calib/
│   ├── intrinsics/         *.yaml
│   └── extrinsics/         *.yaml
├── bags/supplemental.bag
├── imu.csv
├── uwb.csv
└── reference_original.csv
Camera
cam0–cam6 correspond to image_0–image_6. The front pair is image_1 and image_2; fisheye is the sky-pointing camera.
LiDAR
hdl32: Velodyne HDL-32E
xt32: Hesai XT32
GNSS & roadside
Receiver messages and RINEX observations, with the RSI2 LiDAR recordings and roadside GNSS files in roadside/.
Motion & positioning
IMU samples, UWB measurements and the original reference trajectory are provided as separate CSV files.
Calibration
Use the intrinsics and extrinsics supplied inside each sequence's calib/ directory.
